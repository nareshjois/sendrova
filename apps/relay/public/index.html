Built for controlled outbound

One desktop workflow for campaigns — not a hosted CRM.

  • Campaigns Import contacts, edit templates, pause and resume sends. Track progress per list so you can stop mid-run and pick up cleanly later.
  • WhatsApp + SMS Link WhatsApp locally; pair a phone for SMS via the relay. One desktop workflow covers both channels without a hosted inbox.
  • Pacing & caps Rate limits and a shared daily send ceiling you control. Keep outbound steady instead of blasting an entire list at once.
  • Local data SQLite under your profile — no bulk contact upload to us. Campaign lists and send history stay on your machine.
